Details & Summary:
The path-sampling policy is: always sample routes marked with telemetry priority == high, sample medium-priority routes probabilistically at 1%, and use an adaptive (rate-limited with burst tokens) sampling approach for low-priority routes.

- Routes with telemetry priority high are always sampled
- Routes with telemetry priority medium are sampled probabilistically at a 1% rate
- Routes with telemetry priority low use an adaptive sampling approach that is rate-limited with burst tokens

Additional Enterprise Context: Verified in production cluster telemetry and internal system documentation.
