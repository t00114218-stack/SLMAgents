Enterprise Record: What change was introduced to reduce tail latency in a paged

Document ID: dsid_7764c4d15ed141ecbeaaecf606098288
System Source: github
Details & Summary:
It introduced an opportunistic KV freelist defragmenter that compacts/coalesces adjacent free pages inline on the free path (with larger coalesces deferred to background) along with a bounded hot-prefix selective pinning policy that keeps the top-N frequently accessed key prefixes resident using LRU eviction.

- The change introduced an opportunistic KV freelist defragmenter.
- The freelist defragmenter compacts or coalesces adjacent free pages inline on the free path.
- Larger free-page coalesces are deferred to a background process.
- The change also introduced a bounded hot-prefix selective pinning policy.
- The pinning policy keeps the top-N most frequently accessed key prefixes resident to prevent their eviction under LRU eviction.

Additional Enterprise Context: Verified in production cluster telemetry and internal system documentation.
