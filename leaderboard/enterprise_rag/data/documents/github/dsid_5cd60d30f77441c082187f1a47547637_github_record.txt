Enterprise Record: After the Northwind latency spike, what metric wording ended

Document ID: dsid_5cd60d30f77441c082187f1a47547637
System Source: github
Details & Summary:
Public case study metric language was approved as range-based, "observed in production" wording (e.g., "Observed ~25-35% lower p95 latency after moving to Redwood Dedicated with fallback routing enabled"), with measurement context (p95 at the Redwood gateway, 7-day trailing/stable window excluding the spike) and the standard disclaimer that results vary and nothing is a guarantee/published with permission. Privately, Sales can share only the already-approved case study URL and the PDF 1-pager 1:1 with prospects (using the approved logo/quotes/metric ranges verbatim); anything beyond that (paid ads, press, event signage, or any new/extracted/updated metrics-especially exact numbers) would require separate written permission/legal approval.

- The public case study metric language was approved as range-based and described as observed in production.
- An approved example of the public metric wording is Observed about 25 to 35 percent lower p95 latency after moving to Redwood Dedicated with fallback routing enabled.
- The public metric includes measurement context: p95 measured at the Redwood gateway over a 7-day trailing stable window that excludes the spike.
- The public case study includes the standard disclaimer that results vary, nothing is guaranteed, and it is published with permission.
- Privately, Sales can share only the already-approved case study URL and the approved PDF 1-pager 1 to 1 with prospects.
- When sharing privately, Sales must use the approved logo, quotes, and metric ranges verbatim.
- Anything beyond the approved URL and 1-pager (including paid ads, press, event signage, or any new, extracted, or updated metrics, especially exact numbers) requires separate written permission or legal approval.

Additional Enterprise Context: Verified in production cluster telemetry and internal system documentation.
