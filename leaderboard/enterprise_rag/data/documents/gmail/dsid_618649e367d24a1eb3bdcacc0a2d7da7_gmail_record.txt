Details & Summary:
The long bursty token delivery was caused by a memory-pressure cascade on the westus-edge-3 shards: long-context streams plus a recent model change increased per-token KV size, pushing KV cache over a threshold and triggering backpressure that deferred token emission until background eviction cycles (creating 15-30s gaps). As an immediate workaround to keep sessions responsive, Redwood recommended setting a 10s client-side streaming timeout and automatically retrying once (with non-streaming /generate as a fallback for critical flows).
