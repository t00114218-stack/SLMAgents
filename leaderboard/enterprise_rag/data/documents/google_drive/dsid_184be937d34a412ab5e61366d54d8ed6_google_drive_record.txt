Details & Summary:
The draft proposes this canonical v1 signal priority order: (1) authoritative Reachability DOWN → FAILOVER (unless no healthy fallback), (2) fast/severe 5xx error burn → FAILOVER or PARTIAL_SHIFT depending on blast radius and capacity, (3) tail latency burn → PARTIAL_SHIFT first, escalating to FAILOVER if corroborated and sustained, (4) capacity exhaustion/hard gate → HOLD plus load shed (avoid shifting into constrained regions), and (5) control plane degradation → HOLD to avoid rapid flips/oscillation.

- The draft spec proposes a canonical v1 priority order for evaluating failure signals when deciding whether to shift traffic or fail over.
- Highest priority is authoritative Reachability DOWN, which triggers FAILOVER unless there is no healthy fallback.
- Second priority is fast or severe 5xx error burn, which triggers FAILOVER or PARTIAL_SHIFT depending on blast radius and capacity.
- Third priority is tail latency burn, which triggers PARTIAL_SHIFT first and escalates to FAILOVER if corroborated and sustained.
- Fourth priority is capacity exhaustion or a hard gate, which triggers HOLD plus load shed to avoid shifting traffic into constrained regions.
- Fifth priority is control plane degradation, which triggers HOLD to avoid rapid flips or oscillation.
