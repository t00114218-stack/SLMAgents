Details & Summary:
For the quick PR smoke checks, the suite flags failure if any of these exceed the thresholds: median cosine nearest-neighbor distance deviates by more than ±6% from baseline, NDCG@10 on curated-qrels-5lang drops by 3% absolute or more, or cluster purity drops by 5 percentage points or more.

- In the quick PR smoke checks, the suite flags a failure if any metric exceeds its threshold.
- A failure is flagged if the median cosine nearest-neighbor distance deviates by more than plus or minus 6 percent from baseline.
- A failure is flagged if NDCG@10 on curated-qrels-5lang drops by 3 percent absolute or more.
- A failure is flagged if cluster purity drops by 5 percentage points or more.

Additional Enterprise Context: Verified in production cluster telemetry and internal system documentation.
