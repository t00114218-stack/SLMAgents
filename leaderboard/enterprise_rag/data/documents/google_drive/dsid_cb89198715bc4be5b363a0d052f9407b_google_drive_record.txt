Details & Summary:
The adapter's lifecycle states are INIT (boot/verify dependencies), REGISTERING (attempting control-plane join), ACTIVE (serving local traffic), DRAINING (stop new sessions and finish existing), DEGRADED (resource failure; limited service and/or redirected to hosted), and RETIRED (operator shutdown). Key transitions are INIT → REGISTERING → ACTIVE; ACTIVE → DRAINING (operator/rollout) → RETIRED; any state → DEGRADED on resource failure; and DEGRADED → ACTIVE after recovery.

- The edge adapter lifecycle states include INIT, REGISTERING, ACTIVE, DRAINING, DEGRADED, and RETIRED.
- A key transition sequence is INIT to REGISTERING to ACTIVE.
- Another key transition sequence is ACTIVE to DRAINING to RETIRED.
- The transition from ACTIVE to DRAINING can be triggered by an operator action or a rollout.
- Any lifecycle state can transition to DEGRADED on a resource failure.
- In the DEGRADED state, the service provides limited service and or redirects traffic to hosted inference.
- After recovery, the service can transition from DEGRADED back to ACTIVE.
