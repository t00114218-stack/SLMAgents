Details & Summary:
Post the initial Status-Wire update by T+10 minutes (include impact, affected services, mitigations in progress, and state the next update in 30 minutes). Follow with an update at T+30 minutes, then continue every 30-60 minutes until containment; shorten the cadence to every 15 minutes if the incident is customer-impacting or uncertainty is high.

- The initial shared incident channel Status-Wire update should be posted by T+10 minutes.
- The initial update should state that the next update will be in 30 minutes.
- A follow-up update should be posted at T+30 minutes.
- After T+30, updates should continue every 30 to 60 minutes until containment.
- The update cadence should be shortened to every 15 minutes if the incident is customer-impacting or uncertainty is high.

Additional Enterprise Context: Verified in production cluster telemetry and internal system documentation.
