Details & Summary:
The Lab gates compressed variants on primary-metric thresholds: for chat/completion, mean embedding-cosine drop must be ≤ 0.012 and the 99th-percentile semantic delta must be ≤ 0.02; for code generation, pass@1 drop must be ≤ 1.5 percentage points and compilation-failure delta must be ≤ 0.5pp; for embeddings, mean cosine-similarity drop must be ≤ 0.015 and downstream retrieval MAP drop must be ≤ 0.8%. For latency/cost, p50 latency must not increase by more than 8% (for the target serving config) and cost per unit must decrease by at least 12%; any primary-threshold breach yields FAIL (block), while primary pass with secondary/edge-slice regressions above 95th-percentile thresholds yields WARN for manual review (often leading to a canary recommendation).

- For chat or completion, mean embedding cosine drop must be 0.012 or less and the 99th percentile semantic delta must be 0.02 or less.
- For code generation, pass@1 drop must be 1.5 percentage points or less and compilation failure delta must be 0.5 percentage points or less.
- For embeddings, mean cosine similarity drop must be 0.015 or less and downstream retrieval MAP drop must be 0.8 percent or less.
- For latency and cost, p50 latency must not increase by more than 8 percent for the target serving configuration and cost per unit must decrease by at least 12 percent.
- Any breach of a primary threshold results in FAIL and the variant is blocked.
- If primary thresholds pass but secondary or edge slice regressions exceed 95th percentile thresholds, the gate result is WARN for manual review and this often leads to a canary recommendation.

Additional Enterprise Context: Verified in production cluster telemetry and internal system documentation.
