Details & Summary:
Approved wording (Hosted API, EU region): "Redwood Inference supports regional processing for Hosted API workloads. When a customer selects an EU region, Customer Content (request prompts and model outputs) is processed in that region. Access to Customer Content is restricted to authorized personnel on a need-to-know basis and is logged. Certain limited operational metadata (e.g., account and billing information, service health telemetry) may be processed or stored outside the selected region as part of operating and securing the service." For the 'no non-EU support access' demand, use: "Where access is required for support or incident response, it is authorized, time-bound, and logged. Access may be performed by Redwood personnel located outside the EU, depending on the nature of the request and support coverage." If they want EU-only residency / EU-only support access as a contractual commitment, escalate to Legal (Michael Grant) and Security/GRC (Olga Petrov / Daniel Carter).
