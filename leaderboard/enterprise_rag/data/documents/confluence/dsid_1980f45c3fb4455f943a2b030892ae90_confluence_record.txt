Details & Summary:
Throttling was triggered by hot-route protection admission control in us-east: Proxima's bursty combined chat+embeddings traffic exceeded the conservative enterprise route-group burst/budget during a regional pressure window (elevated queue depth/scheduler lag), so requests were denied as "overload_protection_admission/admission_over_budget" with Retry-After 1-2s (not primarily quota). We applied a temporary, region-scoped policy override for PROXIMA-ENT-014: increased the burst multiplier/budget for chat+embeddings in us-east only, time-boxed for 14 days with guardrails/auto-rollback. To verify it's not burning enterprise route SLOs, watch the enterprise (Protected) route x region dashboards for chat/completions and embeddings: error-budget burn/availability and p95/p99 latency stay within SLO targets, 5xx stays below burn thresholds, and enterprise overload/admission-control 429s (which count against enterprise availability) plus enterprise shed_rate remain near zero (alert-level if sustained).
