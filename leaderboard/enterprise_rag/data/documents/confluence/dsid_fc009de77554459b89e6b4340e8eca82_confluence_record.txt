Details & Summary:
At the bundle root (the single directory you extract to), the installer expects these top-level files: `manifest.json` (required), `manifest.sig` (required for signed/normal bundles; only optional for explicitly approved break-glass bundles), and `README.txt` (required).

- The installer checks the bundle root (the single top-level directory you extract to) for required items before accepting the package.
- manifest.json must exist at the top level of the extracted bundle and is required.
- manifest.sig must exist at the top level for signed or normal bundles, and is only optional for explicitly approved break-glass bundles.
- README.txt must exist at the top level of the extracted bundle and is required.
