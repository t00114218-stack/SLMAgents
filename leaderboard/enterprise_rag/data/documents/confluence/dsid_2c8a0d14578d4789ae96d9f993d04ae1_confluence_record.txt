Enterprise Record: What are the current suppression rules and cooldown windows 

Document ID: dsid_2c8a0d14578d4789ae96d9f993d04ae1
System Source: confluence
Details & Summary:
Current PLG task suppression is evaluated before any HubSpot task create and is enforced again in HubSpot via suppression/opt-out fields. Cooldowns (per company + signal_type) block creating another task until: reliability (429/latency) ≥7d, scale/spend thresholds ≥14d, security/compliance intent (SOC2/VPC/Private) ≥30d; intent is also config-driven and defaults to ~14d, with cooldown bypass only on material severity/score increase. Additional suppressions: if an open PLG task exists (ri_plg_signal_task) created in the last 14d, don’t create a new one; if marketing high-touch is active, suppress low-confidence intent tasks; and if customer opt-out/DNC is set, never create tasks and close any open PLG tasks (HubSpot sets a long suppressed_until, e.g., 180d). After Quartz Commerce, intent-only (INTENT_*) is specifically guarded: it cannot create a HubSpot task unless (a) confidence ≥0.85 AND explicit_request=true, or (b) intent is combined with a stronger threshold/supporting product signal (tokens/spend/429s/team expansion etc.); docs-intent-only also needs minimum volume (e.g., 2+ distinct sessions over 48h or intent + a supporting product event). There’s also a 30d suppression after a false_positive disposition. These rules prevent Quartz-style “just browsing” intent from enrolling the ‘create task’ workflow, so the router only updates properties/notes (or just metrics) and HubSpot sees suppressed_until/opt-out and does not create tasks.

- PLG task suppression is evaluated before any HubSpot task creation and is also enforced in HubSpot via suppression or opt-out fields.
- Cooldowns are tracked per company and signal_type and prevent creating another task until the cooldown expires.
- Reliability signals (e.g., 429s or latency) have a cooldown of at least 7 days.
- Scale or spend threshold signals have a cooldown of at least 14 days.
- Security or compliance intent signals (e.g., SOC2, VPC, Private) have a cooldown of at least 30 days.
- Intent signal cooldown is configuration-driven and defaults to about 14 days, with bypass only when there is a material severity or score increase.
- If an open PLG task (ri_plg_signal_task) exists and was created in the last 14 days, a new task is not created.
- If marketing high-touch is active, low-confidence intent tasks are suppressed.
- If customer opt-out or DNC is set, PLG tasks are never created and any open PLG tasks are closed, with HubSpot setting a long suppressed_until (e.g., 180 days).
- After Quartz Commerce, intent-only signals (INTENT_*) cannot create a HubSpot task unless confidence is at least 0.85 and explicit_request is true, or the intent is combined with a stronger supporting product signal (e.g., tokens, spend, 429s, team expansion).
- Docs-intent-only requires minimum volume (e.g., 2+ distinct sessions over 48 hours) or intent plus a supporting product event before creating a task.
- A false_positive disposition triggers a 30-day suppression.
- These rules prevent intent-only browsing from enrolling the create-task workflow by ensuring the router only updates properties or notes while HubSpot sees suppressed_until or opt-out and does not create tasks.

Additional Enterprise Context: Verified in production cluster telemetry and internal system documentation.
