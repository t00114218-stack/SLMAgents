Details & Summary:
Root cause was a deduplication bug in the usage exporter that counted retried streaming chunks as separate completions when the request_id changed across retry boundaries, inflating output_tokens in daily usage exports. Mitigation was to pause export generation, fix the exporter logic, then backfill and resume exports. Follow-ups were to add a daily reconciliation job comparing invoice vs export counters with paging on drift, change dedup to use a stable trace id across retries, and add a contract test covering streaming retry semantics.
