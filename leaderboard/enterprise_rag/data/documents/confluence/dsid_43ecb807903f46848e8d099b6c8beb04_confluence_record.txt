Snapshots are retained in an in-memory local cache for 30 seconds, then forwarded to an indexed store with 7-day retention. Snapshots older than 7 days can be kept in a cold archive for up to 90 days if they are pinned (e.g., by a runbook or regression alert).

- Snapshots are retained in an in-memory local cache for 30 seconds.
- After 30 seconds, snapshots are forwarded to an indexed store with 7-day retention.
- Snapshots older than 7 days can be kept in a cold archive for up to 90 days if they are pinned (for example by a runbook or regression alert).
