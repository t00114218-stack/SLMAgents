Details & Summary:
A quick Deferred Postmortem (DPM) is acceptable when the incident is single-tenant and had less than 30 minutes of customer-visible impact (with a known cause and effective mitigation applied at resolution). If it impacted multiple tenants (e.g., multi-tenant Sev2) or is Sev1, a full postmortem is required.

- A quick Deferred Postmortem is acceptable when the incident is single-tenant and had less than 30 minutes of customer-visible impact.
- A quick Deferred Postmortem is acceptable only if the cause is known and an effective mitigation was applied at resolution.
- If an incident impacted multiple tenants, a full postmortem is required.
- If an incident is Sev1, a full postmortem is required.
