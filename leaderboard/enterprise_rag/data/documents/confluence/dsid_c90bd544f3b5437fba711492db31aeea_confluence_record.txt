Updated PRD thresholds: (a) immediate throttle if any safety sentinel shows >0.3% absolute regression sustained for 5 minutes; (b) automatic cohort-level divert to the previous stable variant if composite quality score drops >3% sustained for 10 minutes in that cohort; (c) full rollback if business KPI regression >4% sustained for 10 minutes. The earlier draft used (a) >0.5% immediate safety-sentinel regression, (b) an automatic 50% traffic revert (not cohort divert) for >3% quality drop sustained 10 minutes, and (c) full rollback for >5% KPI regression for 5 minutes.

- The answer must state the updated safety-sentinel trigger as >0.3% absolute regression sustained for 5 minutes and that it triggers an immediate throttle.
- The answer must state the updated composite quality trigger as >3% drop sustained for 10 minutes and that the action is cohort-level divert to the previous stable variant (not a generic traffic revert).
- The answer must state the updated business KPI trigger as >4% regression sustained for 10 minutes and that it triggers a full rollback.
- If the answer mentions older thresholds, it must label them as earlier/outdated and include: safety sentinel >0.5% (immediate throttle), quality drop >3% for 10 minutes leading to an automatic 50% traffic revert, and KPI regression >5% for 5 minutes leading to full rollback.
- The answer must not present the older KPI threshold (>5% for 5 minutes) as the current policy.
- The answer must not claim the current safety-sentinel threshold is >0.5% or that the current KPI threshold is >5% for 5 minutes.
- The answer must not introduce additional rollback triggers or thresholds (e.g., latency/token cost thresholds) that are not part of the listed automated rollback triggers.
