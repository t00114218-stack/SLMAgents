The slowdown was driven by a rack-4 top-of-rack network issue-an uplink in the LACP group (Gi1/0/24) was flapping/noisy after a config push, causing packet loss/connection breaks that led to high same-rack latency and NCCL allreduce timeouts. The ToR symptom pointing to this was Gi1/0/24 showing heavy RX CRC errors and RX drops along with repeated port-flap alerts.
