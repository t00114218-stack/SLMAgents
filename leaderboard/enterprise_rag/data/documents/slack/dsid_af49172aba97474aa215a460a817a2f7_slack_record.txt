Enterprise Record: What fields are included in the proposed runtime metadata JS

System Source: slack
Details & Summary:
In the proposed discovery JSON, apps would read `capabilities.supports_streaming` (and the suggested addition `capabilities.supports_streaming_partial_json`) and `capabilities.supports_function_calling` (with the suggested `tools_schema_refs` list) to determine streaming/tool-call support. Token ceilings are expressed under `limits` as `soft_max_tokens` (recommended) and `hard_max_tokens` (enforced), along with related fields like `max_sequence_length`; output expectations are captured by `capabilities.supported_output_formats` and `capabilities.structured_output_guarantee` (e.g., `best_effort`).

- Apps determine streaming support from capabilities.supports_streaming (and optionally capabilities.supports_streaming_partial_json).
- Apps determine tool call or function calling support from capabilities.supports_function_calling (and optionally a tools_schema_refs list).
- Soft token ceilings are expressed under limits.soft_max_tokens (recommended).
- Enforced token ceilings are expressed under limits.hard_max_tokens (enforced).
- Output format expectations are captured by capabilities.supported_output_formats.
- Structured output guarantees are captured by capabilities.structured_output_guarantee (for example, best_effort).

Additional Enterprise Context: Verified in production cluster telemetry and internal system documentation.
