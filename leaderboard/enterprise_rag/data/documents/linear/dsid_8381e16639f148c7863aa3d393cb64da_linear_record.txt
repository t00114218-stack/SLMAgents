Details & Summary:
The root cause was overlapping CSS box-shadows-one coming from the component's hover token and another from an outer focus-ring theme variable-briefly stacking into a double outline/glow on icon-only/compact buttons. The proposed fix was to move both treatments into theme variables and avoid stacked shadows by expressing focus as an outline (instead of an additional shadow) for compact/icon buttons.

- The hover double-outline or glow on small icon-style buttons was caused by overlapping CSS box-shadows briefly stacking together.
- One box-shadow came from the component hover token and the other came from an outer focus-ring theme variable.
- The proposed change was to prevent stacked shadow artifacts by expressing focus as an outline instead of an additional shadow for compact or icon-only buttons.
