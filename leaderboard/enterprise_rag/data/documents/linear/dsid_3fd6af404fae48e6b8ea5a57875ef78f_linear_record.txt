Details & Summary:
The acceptance criteria are: (1) deliver a stable design JSON token spec including the base palette, interactive derivative tokens (e.g., color.interactive.primary.hover), and elevation.kappa.0-6 with explicit shadow parameters; (2) land an implementation-ready tokens PR in the frontend repo, with DataGrid and Table consuming the new tokens behind a feature flag; (3) pass accessibility contrast checks for all interactive states (3:1 where applicable and 4.5:1 for primary actionable text); (4) add per-component visual regression snapshots/tolerances with no regressions beyond approved deltas; (5) update the Figma design kit with tokenized components and guidance notes; and (6) execute a rollout plan from internal canary on analytics dashboards to staged rollout and full migration with a fallback.
