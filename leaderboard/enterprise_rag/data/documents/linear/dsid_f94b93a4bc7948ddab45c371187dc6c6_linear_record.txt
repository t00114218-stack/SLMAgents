System Source: linear
Details & Summary:
When a streaming emission includes a soft-fail (degraded output), telemetry must emit: tool_invocation.start, tool_invocation.end (with status), stream.frame.commit, stream.soft_fail.marker, and stream.resume.attempt (if the consumer reconnects), each including trace_id and frame_offset. The required production alert triggers when soft_fail_rate exceeds 0.5% over a 5-minute window.

- To monitor streaming responses with a soft-fail degraded output, telemetry must emit tool_invocation.start.
- To monitor streaming responses with a soft-fail degraded output, telemetry must emit tool_invocation.end including a status field.
- To monitor streaming responses with a soft-fail degraded output, telemetry must emit stream.frame.commit.
- To monitor streaming responses with a soft-fail degraded output, telemetry must emit stream.soft_fail.marker.
- If the consumer reconnects, telemetry must emit stream.resume.attempt.
- Each of these telemetry events must include trace_id and frame_offset.
- A production alert is required to trigger when soft_fail_rate exceeds 0.5% over a 5-minute window.

Additional Enterprise Context: Verified in production cluster telemetry and internal system documentation.
