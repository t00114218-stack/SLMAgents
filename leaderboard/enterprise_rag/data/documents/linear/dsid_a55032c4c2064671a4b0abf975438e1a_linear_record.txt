Details & Summary:
This doc describes implementing a compact, query-friendly benchmark/time-series result store plus a lightweight comparison canvas UI and an operator-focused anomaly triage playbook. It proposes an index-first design (compact columnar time-series index with a cold blob store for raw traces), supports fast p50/p95/tokens/sec lookups and top regression queries, and adds a React comparison canvas for pairwise time-aligned overlays, delta histograms, and deltas by model variant/quantization/hardware.

For repeated-issue grouping, it uses fingerprint-based regression grouping (prompt hashing plus exemplar embeddings, via a 64-bit locality-preserving hash plus a sample embedding signature) to reduce duplicate alerts. Retention is high-resolution traces for 30 days and aggregated rollups for 1 year (with a review suggestion to retain raw KV-cache hit logs for 7 days), and the triage playbook includes RBAC owners, severity mapping, automatic triage suggestions, and links to infra metrics like GPU utilization and kernel selection logs.
