The default token-grace window is 45 seconds, during which a valid resume token is accepted on the destination region before full authentication revalidation.

- The default token grace window during failover is 45 seconds.
- During this 45 second window, a valid short lived resume credential is accepted on the destination region before full authentication is performed again.
