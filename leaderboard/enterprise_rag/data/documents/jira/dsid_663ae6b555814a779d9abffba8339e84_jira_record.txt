Details & Summary:
Root cause: the policy evaluator's not_in operator coerced an absent entitlements claim into a single empty-string value, so the membership checks all returned false and the overall exclusion expression evaluated true, granting access. Immediate workaround: ensure JWTs always include entitlements as an empty array (and/or add an explicit exists(user.entitlements) check and deny when it's missing). Server-side fix: update the evaluator to treat missing claims as an empty set for not_in and remove the absent->empty-string coercion; canary to staging on 2026-03-15 with rollout/patch bundle for private control-plane customers on 2026-03-18.

- The policy evaluator not_in operator coerced a missing entitlements claim into a single empty-string value.
- Because of the empty-string coercion, not_in membership checks returned false and the exclusion expression evaluated true, granting access.

- The server-side fix was to treat missing claims as an empty set for not_in and remove the absent-to-empty-string coercion.
- The fix was canaried to staging on 2026-03-15.
- The rollout or patch bundle for private control-plane customers was on 2026-03-18.
