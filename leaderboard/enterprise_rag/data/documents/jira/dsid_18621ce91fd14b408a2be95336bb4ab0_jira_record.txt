Enterprise Record: What caused the spike in 502 and 503 errors on the embedding

Document ID: dsid_18621ce91fd14b408a2be95336bb4ab0
System Source: jira
Details & Summary:
The 502/503 spike was driven by API gateway backpressure and hedged retries amplifying load on the us-east dedicated pool (dsp-6), which led the serving runtime to exhaust its process/thread limits (pthread_create failures, SIGABRT exits). A recent increase to prefetch.window_size further increased work and contributed to the overload.

- The spike in 502 and 503 errors on the embeddings endpoint for the us-east dedicated pool was driven by API gateway backpressure and hedged retries that amplified load.
- The load amplification impacted the us-east dedicated pool dsp-6.
- The serving runtime exhausted its process or thread limits, leading to pthread_create failures and SIGABRT exits.
- A recent increase to prefetch.window_size increased work and contributed to the overload.

Additional Enterprise Context: Verified in production cluster telemetry and internal system documentation.
