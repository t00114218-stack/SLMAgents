Details & Summary:
It was caused by the identity-proxy caching the user's role/group assertions keyed by the stable SAML session_index, so right after the IdP group sync already-logged-in users could hit a stale cached role set and get RBAC 403s until they re-authenticated. We mitigated by doing targeted session revocations and temporarily lowering the session-index role cache TTL (e.g., 60s to 5s), and the long-term fix was to evict that cache on identity-change (group sync) webhooks so existing sessions immediately pick up new roles.

- The intermittent RBAC 403s happened because identity-proxy cached users role and group assertions keyed by the stable SAML session_index.
- Right after an IdP group membership sync, already logged in users could hit a stale cached role set and get RBAC 403s until they re-authenticated (logging out and back in).
- Mitigation was to perform targeted session revocations and temporarily lower the session_index role cache TTL (for example from 60 seconds to 5 seconds).
- The long-term server-side fix was to evict the role and group cache on identity-change (group sync) webhooks so existing sessions immediately pick up new roles.
