Details & Summary:
Support recommended staggering the credential (signed-cookie) refreshes by adding randomized jitter-about 30-120 seconds-to the refresh schedule so workers don't all renew at once after a restart and trigger 429 rate limits.

- Support recommended staggering credential (signed-cookie) refreshes by adding randomized jitter to the refresh schedule
- The recommended jitter range was about 30 to 120 seconds
- The goal was to prevent workers from all renewing credentials at once after a restart
- Staggering refreshes was intended to stop 429 too many requests rate limit errors caused by refresh bursts

Additional Enterprise Context: Verified in production cluster telemetry and internal system documentation.
