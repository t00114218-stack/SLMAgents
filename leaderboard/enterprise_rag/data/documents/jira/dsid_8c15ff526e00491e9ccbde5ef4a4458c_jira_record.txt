Enterprise Record: In the March 2026 incident where long-lived Server-Sent Even

Document ID: dsid_8c15ff526e00491e9ccbde5ef4a4458c
System Source: jira
Details & Summary:
Root cause was a race in the sse-gateway after a brief upstream TCP RST where the in-flight terminal end event flush interleaved with scheduled keepalive/heartbeat work, causing duplicated heartbeat frames and sometimes dropping the terminal end-of-stream event when the client resumed quickly. The fix was PR 2147 on the server to make terminal end emission atomic and strictly ordered ahead of heartbeats, and to delay/guard resume acceptance with a short grace window so a reconnect can't interleave with the final flush.

- Root cause was a race in the sse-gateway after a brief upstream TCP RST where the in-flight terminal end event flush could interleave with scheduled keepalive or heartbeat work.
- This race caused duplicated heartbeat frames after reconnect and could drop the terminal end-of-stream event when the client resumed quickly.
- The server-side fix (PR 2147) made terminal end emission atomic and strictly ordered ahead of heartbeats.
- The fix also delayed or guarded resume acceptance with a short grace window so a reconnect could not interleave with the final flush.
