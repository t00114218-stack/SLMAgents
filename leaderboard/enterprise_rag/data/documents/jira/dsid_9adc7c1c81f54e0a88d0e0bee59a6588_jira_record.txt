- The edge rate-shedding policy in prod-us-east did not explicitly exempt pinned sessions.
- During the surge, the edge proxy marked some traffic with a shed tag.
- The routing layer's shed-handling logic deprioritized the pinned route and reordered routing candidates based on capacity and SLOs.
- Because of this, requests that were supposed to be pinned sometimes got routed to the gpt-large-v1 canary (experimental fallback) instead of the pinned gpt-large-v1.
