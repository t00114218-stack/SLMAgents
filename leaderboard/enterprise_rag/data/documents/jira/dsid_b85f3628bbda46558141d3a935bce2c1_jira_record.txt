Details & Summary:
During the tier promotion there's a short transition window where rate-limiter refill/priority state isn't fully synchronized (an epoch swap caused ~45s refill-assignment lag, and priority weight recalculation is asynchronous). Background flush workers that spin up immediately after the upgrade can temporarily retain an oversized share of the token pool and grab the newly increased burst credits first, draining availability so high-priority chat/SSE routes start returning 429 quota_exhausted within a few minutes.

- During a tier promotion there can be a short transition window where rate-limiter refill and priority state is not fully synchronized
- An epoch swap during promotion can cause about a 45 second lag in refill assignment
- Priority weight recalculation during promotion is asynchronous
- Background flush or catch-up workers can spin up immediately after the upgrade and temporarily retain an oversized share of the rate-limit token pool
- Those background workers can consume the newly increased burst credits first, draining availability so high-priority real time chat or streaming routes return 429 quota_exhausted within a few minutes

Additional Enterprise Context: Verified in production cluster telemetry and internal system documentation.
