Details & Summary:
The low-risk mitigation was to avoid global changes or restarts by taking a snapshot of BrightHealth's current optimizer profile, then "soft-pausing" per-tenant optimizations via the tenant-gating API by disabling KV prefetch and the batching wait behavior for that tenant. In parallel, they applied a traffic-split rule sending 10% of requests to a stable profile (smaller batch size, kv_prefetch=false, quant_profile=latency-safe) while keeping 90% on the current profile, monitored 5/15/95/99p latencies for ~30 minutes, and then progressively ramped the stable profile share (25% → 50% → 100%) if metrics improved, reverting if any regression appeared.

- They monitored 5th, 15th, 95th, and 99th percentile latencies for about 30 minutes after applying the traffic split.
