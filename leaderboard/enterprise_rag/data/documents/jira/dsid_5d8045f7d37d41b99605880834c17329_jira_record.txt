Details & Summary:
The spike was caused by a KV prefix cache miss storm in the us-east dedicated pool that drove CPU pressure and triggered a router backpressure safety path, which began resetting long-lived streaming connections (truncating replies). Clients then retried, and an idempotency gap in the billing ingestion/ack pipeline for early-terminated streams led to duplicate acknowledgement events and a brief overcounting of usage charges.

- The March 2026 morning spike was caused by a KV prefix cache miss storm in the us-east dedicated pool.
- The cache miss storm drove CPU pressure and triggered a router backpressure safety path.
- The router backpressure safety path reset long-lived streaming connections, cutting off live generated replies mid stream.
- Client retries plus an idempotency gap in the billing ingestion/ack pipeline for early-terminated streams caused duplicate acknowledgement events.
- The duplicate acknowledgement events briefly overcounted usage charges.
