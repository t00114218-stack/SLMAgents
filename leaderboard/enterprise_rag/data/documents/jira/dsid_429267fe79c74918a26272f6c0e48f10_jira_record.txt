Details & Summary:
The jobs failed because an Okta Conditional Access policy change (policy pol-2026-02-27-7) mistakenly applied device_trust enforcement to the service-account group "/Services/NonInteractive," causing Okta to revoke the non-interactive refresh tokens and return 400 invalid_grant during token refresh.

- A change to an Okta Conditional Access policy caused the nightly evaluation cron jobs in the production us-east cluster to fail.
- The specific policy change was Okta Conditional Access policy pol-2026-02-27-7.
- The policy change mistakenly applied device_trust enforcement to the service-account group /Services/NonInteractive.
- Applying device_trust enforcement to the non-interactive service accounts caused Okta to revoke their refresh tokens.
- During token refresh, Okta returned a 400 invalid_grant error due to the revoked refresh tokens.

Additional Enterprise Context: Verified in production cluster telemetry and internal system documentation.
