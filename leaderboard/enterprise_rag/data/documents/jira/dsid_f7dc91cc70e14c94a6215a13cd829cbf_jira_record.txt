Details & Summary:
Root cause: during the 2026-03-09 GPG key rotation, some prod release runners imported a malformed/truncated public key because the internal keysync service mishandled chunked-transfer responses from the internal keyserver, leaving runners with bad/outdated key material; combined with drift in GPG versions/keyrings across runner images this produced BAD_SIGNATURE/invalid packet errors during verification. Immediate mitigations: temporarily roll back the signing host to the previous signing key and, in parallel, unblock hotfix promotions by targeting a small set of prod runners with a forced keyring refresh/import of the correct public key (and temporarily allowing verification with the previous key on those runners) while the keysync fix and broader key rollout proceeded.

- During the 2026-03-09 GPG key rotation, some prod release runners imported a malformed or truncated public key.
- The malformed key was caused by the internal keysync service mishandling chunked-transfer responses from the internal keyserver.
- Bad or outdated key material on runners, combined with drift in GPG versions and keyrings across runner images, produced BAD_SIGNATURE and invalid packet errors during artifact verification.
- An immediate mitigation was to temporarily roll back the signing host to the previous signing key.
- Hotfix promotions were unblocked by targeting a small set of prod runners with a forced keyring refresh to import the correct public key and temporarily allowing verification with the previous key on those runners while the permanent fix rolled out.
