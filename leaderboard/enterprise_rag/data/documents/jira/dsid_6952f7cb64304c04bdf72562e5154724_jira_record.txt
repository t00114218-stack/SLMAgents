- A wrapper was added in the SCCM task sequence before the provisioning step to prevent the Windows 11 imaging task sequence from failing.
- The wrapper forcibly sets the locale variables LANG and LC_COLLATE to en_US.UTF-8 before running the provisioning step.
- Setting LANG and LC_COLLATE to en_US.UTF-8 prevents the setup_local_policies.sh provisioning script failure under legacy or non-default locale collation settings.
